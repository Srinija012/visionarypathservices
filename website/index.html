Operating Transparency

WE DON'T SELL LOANS.
WE HELP YOU UNDERSTAND THEM.

Visionary Path Services operates as an independent education-loan advisory and assistance entity, while final loan decisions remain strictly with RBI-regulated partner lenders.

What Visionary Path Services Helps With

  • Profile evaluation & eligibility assessment across multi-bank schemes
  • Objective comparison between secured and unsecured loan routes
  • Organizing academic, KYC, and financial documentation correctly
  • Coordinating application submission directly with loan officers
  • Follow-up through sanction letters and university disbursement

What Partner Lenders Decide

  • Final credit underwriting, approval, or rejection decisions
  • Final interest rate benchmark assignment & loan margins
  • Applicable administrative processing fees, GST & property valuation
  • Official disbursement schedule & tranche releases to institutions
  • Collateral legal vetting & mortgage documentation

Regulatory Transparency & Operating Model: Visionary Path Services operates as an independent education loan consulting and assistance entity. We do not act as a direct lender, banking institution, or Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC). We do not collect loan repayments, nor do we issue loan sanctions directly. All loan sanctions, interest rate assignments, margin money decisions, and disbursement schedules remain at the sole and independent discretion of our partner RBI-regulated Scheduled Commercial Banks and NBFCs.
