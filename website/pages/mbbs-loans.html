FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About MBBS Loans

Clear answers regarding medical education financing, collateral requirements, and disbursement.

Can I get an education loan for MBBS in private medical colleges in India?
Yes, leading public sector banks, private banks, and NBFCs provide education loans for recognized private medical colleges in India. Depending on fee brackets (which often exceed ₹40 Lakhs), loans may be secured with tangible collateral or provided under special doctor loan schemes.
Is collateral mandatory for MBBS loans abroad?
For MBBS programs abroad exceeding ₹7.5 Lakhs in public banks, tangible collateral (residential/commercial property, fixed deposits) is generally required. However, select NBFCs and private partners offer partially collateralized or non-collateral options up to ₹40-50 Lakhs depending on the financial co-applicant's income and credit score.
Does the loan cover living expenses, hostel fees, and equipment?
Yes, comprehensive education loans cover tuition fees, examination fees, library/laboratory charges, hostel & mess accommodation, books, medical equipment/instruments, and health insurance for the entire 5 to 6 year course duration.
When does repayment start for an MBBS education loan?
Repayment comes with a moratorium period (course duration + 6 months to 1 year internship). During the moratorium, simple interest or partial simple interest is typically serviced, and principal EMI repayments begin after the moratorium ends.
What is Visionary Path Services' service fee for loan processing?
Visionary Path Services provides 100% free guidance and processing assistance to students and parents. We do not charge any service fees or consultation charges from students.
