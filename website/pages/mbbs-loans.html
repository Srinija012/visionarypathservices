PROGRAM OVERVIEW

What Is an MBBS Education Loan?

An MBBS education loan is a specialized academic financing solution structured for undergraduate medical students pursuing degrees in India or recognized institutions abroad.

Because medical education requires 5 to 6 years of intensive training, these loans offer extended repayment horizons, higher funding ceilings, and moratorium periods covering both the academic tenure and mandatory medical internships.

Funding can support eligible expenses such as tuition, accommodation, books, travel and other approved education costs.

LOAN OPTIONS

Secured & Unsecured MBBS Loan Options

Choose the financing structure best suited to your admission tier, institution location, and family financial profile.

Tangible Security

Secured MBBS Education Loan

High-quantum medical education loan backed by tangible collateral such as residential property, commercial premises, or fixed deposits. Enables higher sanction limits, lower interest rates, and longer repayment tenures.

Suitable for: High-fee programs in premier Indian private colleges and leading global universities requiring ₹15 Lakhs up to ₹1.5+ Crores.
Collateral-Free

Unsecured MBBS Education Loan

Collateral-free medical financing evaluated on student academic merit, NEET ranking, and the financial co-borrower's verifiable monthly salary and credit bureau record.

Suitable for: Government medical college admissions and approved global institutions requiring ₹7.5 Lakhs up to ₹40 Lakhs without pledging physical assets.

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility may depend on your academic profile, institution, course and financial requirements.

Academic Background

Indian national with minimum 50% marks in 10+2 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) and valid qualifying NEET score.

Recognized College/Institution

Admission confirmed in an NMC (National Medical Commission), WHO, or government-approved medical institution.

Course/Program

Enrolled in a regular full-time undergraduate MBBS, MD, or recognized equivalent medical degree.

Funding Requirement

Official fee breakdown issued by the college specifying tuition fees, hostel, clinical deposits, and other charges.

Co-Applicant Profile

Resident Indian financial co-borrower (parent, spouse, sibling, or guardian) with verifiable salary or business income.

Credit History

Healthy repayment track record with satisfactory CIBIL or credit bureau score for the primary financial co-signer.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About MBBS Loans

Clear answers regarding medical education financing, collateral requirements, and disbursement.

Can I get an education loan for MBBS in private medical colleges in India?
Yes, leading public sector banks, private banks, and NBFCs provide education loans for recognized private medical colleges in India. Depending on fee brackets (which often exceed ₹40 Lakhs), loans may be secured with tangible collateral or provided under specialized medical student lending schemes.
Is collateral mandatory for MBBS loans abroad?
For MBBS programs abroad exceeding ₹7.5 Lakhs in public banks, tangible collateral (residential/commercial property, fixed deposits) is generally required. However, select NBFCs and private banking partners offer partially collateralized or non-collateral options up to ₹40–₹50 Lakhs depending on the financial co-applicant's income and credit score.
What expenses are covered under an MBBS education loan?
Comprehensive education loans cover 100% tuition fees, examination and laboratory charges, hostel and living accommodation, medical books, diagnostic equipment, health insurance, and mandatory travel expenses for the entire 5 to 6 year course duration.
Do I need a co-applicant for an MBBS loan?
Yes, a financial co-applicant (typically a parent, spouse, sibling, or legal guardian) is mandatory for education loans in India. The co-applicant's verifiable income, banking history, and credit score (CIBIL) determine loan eligibility and sanction limits.
When does repayment start for an MBBS education loan?
Repayment begins with a generous moratorium period that covers the entire 5 to 6 years of academic study plus 6 months to 1 year of compulsory rotating internship. During the moratorium, simple interest or partial interest is serviced, and full principal EMI installments begin after course completion.
