Critical Protective Notice: Interest Rates & Bank Modifications

Interest rates are strictly subject to partner banks and NBFCs and can be modified, adjusted, or revised at any time without prior notice. All interest rates, loan limits, moratorium periods, and processing fees displayed on this website, in marketing materials, or communicated during advisory consultations are indicative benchmarks. Final sanction terms depend on individual borrower creditworthiness, collateral evaluation, university ranking, and lender underwriting policies. *Terms & Conditions Apply.

1. Primary Interest Rate & Sanction Disclaimer

Visionary Path Services ("VPS") provides educational finance consultation, eligibility comparisons, and documentation facilitation.

Any interest rates mentioned across our portal (such as starting benchmark rates of 8.5% p.a. for public sector banks or 10.5% p.a. for private NBFCs) represent prevailing base rates or indicative market ranges compiled for comparative analysis. These figures do not constitute a contractual offer, a guaranteed rate, or an official bank quotation.

  • Floating Rate Benchmark: Most educational loans in India are sanctioned under floating rate mechanisms linked to external benchmarks (such as the RBI External Benchmark Lending Rate / Repo Rate [EBLR/RLLR] or lender Marginal Cost of Funds Based Lending Rate [MCLR]). Fluctuations in the RBI Repo Rate will cause interest rates to adjust upwards or downwards over the loan tenure.
  • Risk-Based Pricing: Individual rates are determined by the lending institution’s credit committee after rigorous evaluation of the student’s academic credentials, entrance test percentiles, co-applicant CIBIL score, FOIR, family net worth, and target university tier.

2. Sole and Absolute Discretion of Partner Banks & NBFCs

All decisions regarding education loan applications—including but not limited to:

  1. Loan application acceptance, initial screening, and pre-qualification;
  2. Final loan approval, sanction amount, and sanction validity period;
  3. Interest rate assignment, spread, margin, and type (fixed vs floating);
  4. Required margin money contribution (self-funding component);
  5. Acceptability of collateral security and property legal title clearance;
  6. Processing fee percentages, upfront administrative fees, and loan cancellation charges;
  7. Sanction rejection or cancellation of undisbursed loan tranches;

remain at the sole, absolute, and unfettered discretion of the respective partner bank or NBFC. Visionary Path Services exercises zero authority or control over credit committee decisions and cannot be held responsible for loan rejections, delays, or reductions in requested sanction ceilings.

3. Non-Lender / Intermediary Status

Statutory Disclosure: Visionary Path Services is an independent education loan advisor and Direct Selling Agent (DSA). Visionary Path Services is NOT a Bank, Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), or licensed lending institution under the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

VPS does not accept deposits, underwrite loans, hold borrower funds, or disburse loans directly to students or educational universities. All loan contracts, sanction letters, Key Fact Statements (KFS), promissory notes, and loan disbursements are executed exclusively between the borrower and the authorized institutional lender.

4. Financial Calculators: Illustrative Self-Help Tools Only

The financial tools and calculators available on our website—including the:

  • Education Loan EMI Calculator: Calculates estimated monthly payments using mathematical annuity formulas based on user-entered values;
  • INR / USD Currency & Budget Planner: Calculates indicative living and tuition costs based on estimated forex exchange benchmarks;
  • Loan Takeover / Refinancing Calculator: Models potential interest savings based on hypothetical rate differentials;
  • Section 80E Tax Deduction Calculator: Estimates potential tax savings under Section 80E of the Indian Income Tax Act;
  • Loan Eligibility Checker: Provides estimated borrowing capacity based on typical lender parameters;

are provided strictly as interactive self-help tools for preliminary illustrative and educational purposes. They do not constitute an official loan sanction, pre-approval, financial quote, or professional financial advice. Actual EMIs, taxes, forex rates, and sanctioned loan limits will vary based on exact bank underwriting terms.

6. Tax Law & Section 80E Disclaimers

Information provided regarding tax deductions on education loan interest under Section 80E of the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961 is for general informational awareness only.

  • Tax deduction benefits depend on individual tax regime selections (Old Tax Regime vs. New Tax Regime under Section 115BAC), taxable income thresholds, and specific eligibility requirements (loan borrowed from an approved financial institution for higher education).
  • Visionary Path Services does not provide formal chartered accountancy or certified tax advisory services. Applicants and co-borrowers are strongly advised to consult a qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) or tax professional before making tax filings.

7. Foreign Exchange (Forex) & Currency Remittance Disclaimers

Tuition and living expense conversions between Indian Rupees (INR) and foreign currencies (USD, GBP, CAD, EUR, AUD, etc.) are based on indicative forex rates.

Actual tuition remittances, wire transfers, Forex card charges, currency markup fees, and Tax Collected at Source (TCS) under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) are subject to RBI guidelines and prevailing real-time spot rates at the exact date and hour of execution by authorized AD-II category forex dealers or partner banks.

You authorize Visionary Path Services, our certified education loan counselors, and verified partner banks to contact you via telephone call, WhatsApp, SMS, and email. This authorization explicitly overrides any registration of your contact number with the National Customer Preference Register (NCPR) or National Do Not Disturb (DND) database as per Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) directives.
