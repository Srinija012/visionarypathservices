Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from education consultants, DSAs, and institutions exploring partnership with VPS.

Is there any onboarding fee or cost to partner with Visionary Path Services?

No. Partnering with Visionary Path Services is 100% free of charge. There are zero registration fees, onboarding costs, or hidden maintenance charges for any consultancy or DSA partner.

Which lending institutions and banks do you work with?

We work with 15+ premier RBI-regulated Scheduled Commercial Banks, leading NBFCs, and global dollar lenders. Key partners include State Bank of India (SBI), Bank of Baroda, HDFC Credila, Avanse Financial Services, Auxilo Finserve, InCred, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, IDFC First Bank, as well as international lenders Prodigy Finance and MPower Financing for collateral-free foreign study loans.

Can you assist students who require non-collateral (unsecured) loans?

Yes. Non-collateral education loans are one of our core specialties. We assist eligible students in securing unsecured financing up to ₹1.5 Cr+ for premier institutions in the USA, UK, Canada, Ireland, Australia, and India based on academic pedigree, test scores, and co-applicant profile.

How quickly will a loan advisor contact our referred students?

Referred student applications receive priority desk treatment. An assigned Senior Loan Officer connects with the student within 2 to 4 business hours to conduct an initial profile assessment and outline document requirements.

Do students have to pay any fees to Visionary Path Services?

Absolutely zero. Our advisory, eligibility analysis, multi-bank comparison, and filing assistance are completely free for students. Official processing fees and valuation fees (if any) are paid directly to the respective lending banks.
