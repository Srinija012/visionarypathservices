3. Bank Sole Discretion Clause & No Guarantee of Sanction

Visionary Path Services strives to secure the highest approval probability and favorable terms for every applicant. However, we do not and cannot guarantee loan approvals, specific sanctioned amounts, or interest rates.

  • Underwriting Authority: The respective partner bank or NBFC holds the sole, final, and non-negotiable authority to accept, decline, conditionally approve, or revoke any loan application.
  • Credit Assessment Factors: Lender credit committees make decisions independently based on the borrower’s CIBIL/credit score, FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio), target university global ranking, academic standing, collateral legal clearance, and internal risk policies.
  • Collateral Acceptability: For secured loans, property title clearance, non-agricultural status, municipal approval, and valuation ratios are determined solely by the bank's empanelled legal and technical evaluators.

4. Interest Rates, Fees & Market Policy Modifications

Interest Rate Protection & Revision Clause

Interest rates are subject to modification by banks and NBFCs at their discretion. Most Indian education loans are structured on floating rate benchmarks linked to the RBI Repo Rate (EBLR/RLLR) or Marginal Cost of Funds Based Lending Rate (MCLR). Partner lenders reserve the unilateral right to revise interest rates, spreads, and margins at any time prior to or following loan sanction in accordance with prevailing monetary policies and statutory regulations.

Actual interest rates, loan tenure, EMI amortizations, and repayment terms will be explicitly specified only in the formal Sanction Letter and Loan Agreement issued directly by the financing bank or NBFC.
