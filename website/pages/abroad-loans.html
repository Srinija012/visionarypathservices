ABROAD EDUCATION

What is an Abroad Education Loan?

An abroad education loan is financing that can help eligible students meet the cost of higher education outside India. Depending on the lender and applicant profile, the loan may cover various education-related expenses.

Comprehensive Financing Tuition, Living & Travel Expenses

Expenses That Can Be Covered (as per lender policy)

Tuition Fees

Fees payable to the university or institution.

Accommodation & Living Expenses

Eligible accommodation and living costs.

Travel Expenses

Certain eligible travel-related expenses.

Academic Expenses

Books, equipment and other study-related expenses.

Other Education Expenses

Other approved expenses, depending on the lender.

Note: Coverage and eligible expenses vary by lender, course, institution and applicant profile.

LOAN OPTIONS

Secured & Unsecured Education Loans

Choose the option that suits your profile and funding requirement.

Secured Education Loan

Education loan where collateral/security may be required, subject to lender policy.

Suitable for: Higher funding requirements and profiles meeting lender criteria.

Unsecured Education Loan

Education loan where collateral may not be required for eligible profiles, subject to lender criteria.

Suitable for: Eligible students who meet the lender's academic and financial requirements.
Feature / Parameter Secured (Collateral) Loan Unsecured (Non-Collateral) Loan
Maximum Loan Amount Up to ₹2+ Crores (100% of I-20 / CAS costs) Up to ₹75 Lakhs (Subject to GRE & co-applicant)
Interest Rate Benchmark 8.50% – 10.25% p.a. (Floating / Concessional) 10.50% – 12.75% p.a. (Risk-adjusted)
Acceptable Security Residential property, commercial plot, Fixed Deposit (FD) Zero Collateral (Based on academic potential)
Processing Turnaround 10 – 15 business days (Includes legal & technical valuation) 4 – 7 business days (Express digital sanction)
