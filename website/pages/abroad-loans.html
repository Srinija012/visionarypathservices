HOW IT WORKS

Your Education Loan Journey

Step-by-step guidance from initial assessment to final disbursement.

1. Profile Assessment

We evaluate your academic background, target university, country, and financial profile to map the best loan options.

2. Loan Option Guidance

We explain suitable options across public banks, private banks, and NBFCs — both secured and unsecured.

3. Documentation Assistance

Clear checklists and step-by-step help organizing academic, financial, KYC, and collateral papers.

4. Eligibility Guidance

We review co-applicant income, CIBIL standing, and program eligibility before submission to minimize delays.

5. Application Support

Direct application submission and regular follow-ups with bank officers to fast-track your loan file.

6. Sanction & Disbursement

Guidance through sanction letter verification, property valuation, mortgage formalities, and fee remittance.
