Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for a personal loan without providing collateral?
Yes. Personal loans offered by our partner banks and NBFCs are 100% unsecured and do not require any physical asset or collateral security. Eligibility is based on your income, employer profile, and CIBIL credit score.
What is the maximum tenure available for Home Loans?
Home loans offer flexible repayment tenures of up to 30 years depending on the applicant's age and income profile. We help compare EMI plans across major public and private banks to minimize your overall interest outgo.
Can I finance both new and pre-owned (used) vehicles?
Yes, our lending network includes options for both brand new cars and certified pre-owned passenger vehicles, with funding up to 85%–90% of the vehicle value and flexible tenures up to 7 years.
What are the key criteria for Business Loans?
For business loans (working capital, machinery purchase, or expansion), lenders typically review business vintage (minimum 1–2 years), annual turnover, GST returns, and bank statements. Both collateral-free MSME loans and secured options are available.
Does Visionary Path Services charge any upfront advisory fee?
No. Visionary Path Services provides 100% free loan advisory and facilitation. We never charge any upfront fees or consulting commission from our clients.
