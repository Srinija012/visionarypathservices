LOAN CATEGORIES

Domestic Loan Options

We guide you through the most suitable loan category based on your profile, institution, and funding requirement.

🏛️

Public Bank Loans

Government-backed education loans with competitive interest rates, often supported by schemes like CGFSEL and Padho Pardesh.

  • Subsidized interest options available
  • Lower interest rates generally
  • Moratorium period benefits
  • SBI, PNB, Canara Bank, Bank of Baroda
🏦

Private Bank Loans

Faster processing and flexible options from private sector banks for domestic education needs.

  • Quick processing & disbursement
  • Flexible repayment options
  • Higher loan amounts possible
  • ICICI, IDFC, YES Bank, AXIS Bank
💡

NBFC Loans

Non-banking financial companies offering innovative loan products with varied eligibility criteria for domestic courses.

  • Collateral-free options available
  • Flexible credit assessment
  • Tailored for specific course types
  • Credila, Avanse, Auxilo, InCred
