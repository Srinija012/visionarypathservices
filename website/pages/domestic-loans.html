OVERVIEW

What is a Domestic Education Loan?

A domestic education loan helps eligible students meet the cost of higher education in India. Depending on the lender and applicant profile, the loan may cover tuition fees, accommodation, living expenses and other education-related costs.

  • Premier Institute Schemes: Concessional rates for IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, BITS & top NIRF-ranked campuses.
  • Comprehensive Financing: 100% funding with zero margin money required for top-tier Indian colleges.
  • Flexible Moratorium: Repayment begins only after course completion plus an additional 6 to 12 months grace period.
  • Tax Advantages: Avail unlimited income-tax deduction on entire interest paid under Section 80E.

LOAN COMPARISON

Secured & Unsecured Education Loans

Choose the option that suits your profile, target institution, and funding requirement.

With Collateral / Security

Secured Education Loan

Education loan where collateral/security (such as residential property, fixed deposits, or commercial asset) may be pledged, subject to lender policy.

Suitable for: Higher funding requirements (₹20 Lakhs – ₹1.5+ Crore) and students seeking the lowest possible interest rates.
  • Lowest interest rates starting from 8.15% p.a.
  • Extended repayment tenure up to 15 years post-moratorium
  • Maximum sanction amount for high-cost programs
Collateral-Free Financing

Unsecured Education Loan

Education loan where collateral is not required for eligible profiles, approved primarily on academic merit and co-applicant financial strength.

Suitable for: Eligible students attending top-tier institutes (IITs, IIMs, premier NIRF colleges) seeking fast, zero-property approvals.
  • Up to ₹40–50 Lakhs collateral-free for premier colleges
  • Rapid digital processing with 3 to 5 business day TAT
  • Zero property evaluation or mortgage documentation

COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers on domestic loans.

Can I get a loan for any course in India?

Most lenders cover degree, postgraduate, and professional courses from recognized universities (UGC/AICTE/NIRF accredited). Vocational and diploma courses are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Do I need a co-applicant?

Yes, for domestic education loans in India, a parent, guardian, or spouse typically acts as a co-applicant to fulfill lender credit and income criteria.

Can I get an education loan without collateral?

Yes. Students attending premier institutions (IITs, IIMs, NITs, and top private universities) can secure collateral-free loans up to ₹20L – ₹40 Lakhs based on academic merit.

How long does the loan process take?

With our streamlined partner desks, digital verification typically completes in 3 to 7 business days once all documentation is submitted.

Are living and hostel expenses included?

Yes. In addition to college tuition fees, approved hostel accommodation, mess charges, examination fees, and study laptops can be included in the loan sanction.
