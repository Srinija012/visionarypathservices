### Common Questions About MBBS Loans

Clear answers regarding medical education financing, collateral requirements, and disbursement.

Can I get an education loan for MBBS in private medical colleges in India? Yes, leading public sector banks, private banks, and NBFCs provide education loans for recognized private medical colleges in India. Depending on fee brackets (which often exceed ₹40 Lakhs), loans may be secured with tangible collateral or provided under special doctor loan schemes. Is collateral mandatory for MBBS loans abroad? For MBBS programs abroad exceeding ₹7.5 Lakhs in public banks, tangible collateral (residential/commercial property, fixed deposits) is generally required. However, select NBFCs and private partners offer partially collateralized or non-collateral options up to ₹40-50 Lakhs depending on the financial co-applicant's income and credit score. Does the loan cover living expenses, hostel fees, and equipment? Yes, comprehensive education loans cover tuition fees, examination fees, library/laboratory charges, hostel & mess accommodation, books, medical equipment/instruments, and health insurance for the entire 5 to 6 year course duration. When does repayment start for an MBBS education loan? Repayment comes with a moratorium period (course duration + 6 months to 1 year internship). During the moratorium, simple interest or partial simple interest is typically serviced, and principal EMI repayments begin after the moratorium ends. ## Collateral vs Non-Collateral Education Loans: Maximum Limits, Interest Rates & Bank List (2026)

By Visionary Path Research Team Updated September 2026 8 Min Read Bank-Policy Verified

When calculating the cost of studying abroad—where master's degrees in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia cost between ₹30 Lakhs and ₹80+ Lakhs —one pivotal question confronts every family: "Do we need to mortgage our property, or can we secure an education loan without pledging collateral?"

Historically, Indian banks refused higher education loans without immovable property security. Today, however, specialized education NBFCs and aggressive private banks have transformed the lending landscape, offering unsecured education loans up to ₹75 Lakhs for premier programs.

At Visionary Path Services , our goal is to guide you toward the most cost-effective and feasible loan structure based on your family's assets, income, and university ranking. Here is the definitive 2026 breakdown.


#### Secured Loan (With Collateral)

- Interest Rate: 8.50% – 10.25% p.a. (Lower)
- Max Limit: Up to ₹1.5 – ₹2+ Crores
- Turnaround: 18 – 25 working days
- Margin Money: 10% – 15%
- Top Lenders: SBI, Bank of Baroda, Canara, PNB


#### Unsecured Loan (No Collateral)

- Interest Rate: 10.50% – 13.50% p.a.
- Max Limit: Up to ₹50 – ₹75 Lakhs
- Turnaround: 5 – 8 working days (Fast)
- Margin Money: Often 0% (100% funding)
- Top Lenders: HDFC Credila, Avanse, Auxilo, Axis


### Secured Education Loans: What Types of Collateral Are Accepted?

A secured education loan requires the borrower or a third-party guarantor to pledge legal security to the bank. Collateral is broadly categorized into two buckets:


#### 1. Immovable Tangible Property

- Residential Properties: Self-occupied independent houses, villas, or residential apartments/flats with approved municipal sanctioned plans and occupancy certificates.
- Commercial Real Estate: Office spaces, retail shops, or commercial buildings with clear title deeds and commercial tax assessments.
- Non-Agricultural Land: Clear-title, demarcated non-agricultural open plots with municipal boundary approvals. ( Note: Pure agricultural farmland is strictly prohibited as collateral under SARFAESI Act norms for nationalized banks. )


#### 2. Liquid / Financial Collateral

- Fixed Deposits (FDs): Bank fixed deposits held with the lending bank or approved financial institutions. Lenders typically sanction 90% to 100% of the FD face value immediately.
- Government Securities: Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), National Savings Certificates (NSC), or RBI relief bonds.
- Life Insurance Policies: Surrender value of traditional endowment policies from LIC or premier private insurers.


### Non-Collateral Loans: How Do NBFCs Underwrite Them?

Without physical collateral, the lender assumes 100% of the repayment risk. To compensate, underwriters look at three critical pillars:

- University Ranking & Employability: Lenders maintain categorized lists (Tier-1, Tier-2, Tier-3). If you are accepted into a Top 100 US university, ivy-league institute, or premier STEM/MBA program, NBFCs compete to offer unsecured loans up to ₹60–₹75 Lakhs because your post-graduation placement salary is statistically assured.
- Co-Applicant Monthly Cash Flow: Unsecured lenders require the co-applicant (parent or guardian) to have a steady, verifiable monthly income (salaried with Form 16 or self-employed with 3 years ITR) capable of servicing simple or partial interest during the study moratorium.
- Credit Bureau Standing (CIBIL 700+): Clean repayment track without missed installments or settlement marks. (Read our in-depth CIBIL Score for Education Loans Guide ).

## How Does CIBIL Score Affect Your Education Loan Approval? (2026 In-Depth Guide)

By Visionary Path Advisory Team Updated September 2026 7 Min Read Verified Against Bank Guidelines

Securing an admit letter from your dream university in the US, UK, Canada, Germany, or a premier institute in India is a milestone. Yet, for thousands of students each intake, the loan approval process suddenly hits an unexpected roadblock: a low or flagged CIBIL score .

Many students ask us: "I am a fresher and have never taken a loan—do I even have a CIBIL score?" Others face anxiety when their parents' credit reports show an old disputed card bill or loan settlement.

At Visionary Path Services , we evaluate hundreds of student financial profiles monthly. In this definitive guide, we break down how credit bureaus work, exact cutoff thresholds across public banks, private lenders, and NBFCs, and proven solutions to secure your loan even if your CIBIL score is below average.


##### Key Takeaway Before You Read:

For fresh student applicants with no borrowing history, having a score of "-1" (No Credit History) is completely normal and will not cause a rejection. Lenders focus primarily on the co-applicant's (parent/guardian) credit track .


### Understanding CIBIL Scores & Rating Tiers

The TransUnion CIBIL score is a 3-digit score ranging between 300 and 900 that summarises an individual's borrowing behavior, credit repayment consistency, existing liabilities, and credit card usage over the previous 24 to 36 months.

750 – 900 Excellent

Instant pre-approvals, lowest interest rate concessions, maximum sanction limits.

685 – 749 Good / Standard

Widely accepted by top NBFCs (Credila, Avanse) and private banks for unsecured loans.

600 – 684 Borderline / Average

Unsecured loans tough; eligible with public banks against collateral or secondary guarantor.

Below 600 High Risk

High rejection rate on automated platforms. Requires manual credit officer deviation & solid security.


### Whose CIBIL Score Do Banks Actually Scrutinize?

A frequent source of anxiety among applicants is whether the student or parent is evaluated:

- The Student's Profile (Fresher / No History): If you are a college graduate or school pass-out who has never had a credit card, consumer durable EMI, or personal loan, your CIBIL score will show as "-1", "NA", or "NH" (No History) . Banks expect this. It does not hinder your approval because students are in their pre-earning study phase.
- The Student with Prior Work Experience: If you worked for a few years before pursuing your Master's/MBA and used credit cards or personal loans, your score will be pulled . Any default in your name must be addressed before applying.
- The Co-Applicant (The Primary Risk Anchor): Because education loans feature a repayment holiday (moratorium period) during study years, the financial guarantor (Father, Mother, Spouse, or Legal Guardian) is solely responsible for servicing simple interest or handling unforeseen contingencies. Hence, the co-applicant's CIBIL score is the #1 deciding factor for standard education loans.


### CIBIL Score Cutoffs Across Indian Lenders

Requirements differ dramatically based on whether you are pledging collateral (house, flat, fixed deposit) or seeking a non-collateral loan:


| Lender Category | Loan Type | Min. Score Expected | Key Policy Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public Sector Banks (SBI, Bank of Baroda, Canara, PNB) | Secured (With Collateral) | 590 – 650+ | Most forgiving on CIBIL if genuine property collateral & legal title are established. Lowest ROI. |
| Private Banks (Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, HDFC) | Unsecured & Secured | 685 – 700+ | Automated algorithmic credit evaluation. Zero tolerance for overdue payments in the last 6 months. |
| Education NBFCs (HDFC Credila, Avanse, Auxilo, InCred) | Unsecured (Abroad Studies) | 685 – 720+ | Customized higher sanction limits (up to ₹60–₹75L without collateral) for high-ranking STEM/MBA programs. |
| International Lenders (Prodigy Finance, MPOWER) | No Cosigner / USD Loan | No Indian Score Needed | Evaluates university ranking, GRE/GMAT score, and post-graduation earning potential rather than parents' credit. |


### 4 Critical Red Flags on a Credit Report

It is not just the overall 3-digit number that matters. Underwriting managers review the granular statements on the report. Here are the 4 issues that trigger auto-rejection:


##### Red Flags That Cause Immediate Disqualification:

1. "Settled" or "Written-Off" Status: If a past credit card or personal loan was settled for a lower amount instead of paid in full, credit algorithms block the file immediately.

2. High DPDU (Days Past Due): Consecutive 30+, 60+, or 90+ days delay on ongoing home/car loan EMIs within the past 12 months.

3. Frequent Cheque / ECS Bounces: Bank account statements showing recurring inward/outward bounce charges.

4. Over-Leveraged Debt-to-Income (FOIR): If existing EMIs consume more than 60% of the co-applicant's monthly net in-hand salary.

### 5 Proven Workarounds for a Low CIBIL Score

If your parent or financial guarantor has a score below 685, your study abroad dreams are not over. Here are 5 practical, bank-approved solutions we use at Visionary Path Services :


#### 1. Introduce a Secondary Financial Co-Applicant

Most private banks and NBFCs allow adding a second earning co-borrower. If the father's CIBIL is low, adding the mother, an earning elder sibling, or a first-degree relative (paternal/maternal uncle or aunt with clean credit history) balances out the credit risk profile.


#### 2. Switch to a Secured (Collateral) Loan with a PSU Bank

Public Sector Banks like SBI and Bank of Baroda prioritize **tangible security**. Pledging residential property, commercial real estate, or fixed deposits creates an ironclad security cover, enabling branch managers to approve files with CIBIL scores around 600–620 through internal discretionary powers.


#### 3. Pay Off Overdue Balances & Obtain an NDC (No Dues Certificate)

Often, an old forgotten credit card balance of ₹5,000–₹10,000 drags down an entire CIBIL score. Paying the full amount (avoiding settlement concessions) and obtaining an official No Dues Certificate (NDC) or No Objection Certificate (NOC) allows loan officers to seek credit exceptions.


#### 4. Apply for International No-Cosigner Dollar Loans

For students heading to accredited universities in the USA, UK, Canada, or France for STEM and management degrees, specialized global lenders (such as Prodigy Finance or MPOWER) evaluate the student's future employability without requiring an Indian co-applicant or collateral.


#### 5. Profile-Based Lender Routing with Visionary Path Services

Every financial institution operates under a distinct credit risk appetite. A student rejected by an algorithm at Bank A can often be approved at Bank B when the dossier is presented with proper compensating factors (academic merit, university global rank, collateral value).

An education loan is one of the most powerful financial vehicles for young professionals. Once your study period and grace moratorium end, paying your education loan EMIs on time for 12–24 months directly establishes a pristine 780+ CIBIL score in your early twenties.

This prime credit rating will later unlock preferential interest rates when you eventually apply for home loans, auto loans, or startup credit facilities.


### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Does a student need a CIBIL score for an abroad education loan? No. Fresh students with no prior credit history will have a score marked as -1 or NA. Banks consider this neutral. The primary financial evaluation is performed on the co-applicant's credit report and income stability. Can SBI approve an education loan with a CIBIL score of 620? Yes. Under the SBI Global Ed-vantage scheme, if the loan is backed by 100% tangible property collateral and the delay in past repayments is adequately explained, branch managers can process approvals through regional credit committee deviations. What is the minimum CIBIL score for an unsecured loan from NBFCs? Most prominent education NBFCs (including HDFC Credila, Avanse, and Auxilo) prefer a minimum co-applicant CIBIL score of 685. Applicants with scores above 720 enjoy faster processing and lower processing fees. How can I check my CIBIL score without damaging it? Checking your own CIBIL score through authorized bureau portals (soft inquiry) does not impact your credit score. However, submitting multiple formal loan applications to different banks simultaneously creates multiple "hard inquiries," which can lower your score.
